## Product Purpose

Orion Flare sells cotton shirts printed with DTF using the design the customer wants. Today every order arrives as an Instagram/WhatsApp message and the owner has to find or clean up the image by hand. The site lets the customer configure the shirt (type, colour, size, cut), upload the design, clean it (remove background, upscale), place it on a 3D shirt at its real size in cm, see the price and send the order. The owner receives the order with measurements in cm and a print-ready image (300 DPI) and only has to review and print. Success: fewer back-and-forth messages, every order arrives print-ready, and DTF cost is controlled.

## Positioning

A small local maker who lets you design your own shirt on a real-size 3D preview and prints exactly that, with the cleanup (background removal, upscaling) done for you before it reaches the printer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Garment types and sizes come from the supplier's size chart (inches; width = armpit to armpit, length = shoulder to hem): kids, unisex (short/long/sleeveless), women crew neck, women V-neck, crop top, women modern, oversized, polos.
- Largest print so far: ~38 × 45 cm on an oversized 2XL. Max print area depends on size.
- Known costs: oversized 2XL blank $8.25, regular L blank $4.50; DTF 60×100 cm $16.90, 60×70 cm $14.50, 58×40 cm ~$9. Typical selling price has been ~$15. Other blank costs are unknown and must stay editable.
- Must be free to host and run (free tiers only).
- Online card payments: undecided. Stripe does not serve El Salvador merchants; candidates are Wompi, n1co, PayPal, bank transfer or cash on delivery.
- Must comply with: privacy policy, terms, refunds/exchanges, cookies, accessibility, customer declares rights over the uploaded design, local consumer protection and personal-data law.

## Product Principles

1. What the customer sees is what gets printed: real sizes in cm, honest preview, honest price.
2. Every order reaches the owner print-ready; the site does the tedious work.
3. Phone first: most customers come from Instagram on a phone.
4. Free to run; no service that charges per month before the business needs it.
5. Respect the customer's data and the rights of the artwork.
