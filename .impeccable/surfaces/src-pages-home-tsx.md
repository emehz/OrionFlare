## Scope
Home (`/`): Persuade. Configurator (`/crear`): Operate. Legal pages: Read.
Audience: anime/gaming fans and people with their own design, arriving from Instagram on a phone. Action: open the configurator and send a print-ready order.
Brand pinned by PRODUCT.md (black/white, planet-with-ring logo, katakana, torn paper/ink). Direction derived from the brief, no concept roll (brief-pinned world). Code-led: no image generation on this machine.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The shirt is the page. A real-size 3D shirt owns the first viewport and every scroll chapter acts on it; refuses the catalogue grid of product cards.
OWN-WORLD: Sumi black #0B0B0C ground, newsprint white #F2F0EA, one hanko vermilion #E5381E used only for the primary action and the price stamp. Manga screentone dots, speed lines, panel gutters, vertical katakana (tategaki). Dela Gothic One display, Zen Kaku Gothic New text. Brand neck labels and logo from the owner's files.
STORY: The visitor sees their kind of print on a shirt, learns it is printed at real size in cm with cleanup done for them, and taps "Diseñar la mía".
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL shirt centered, slowly turning, wearing a random sample print; giant vertical オリオンフレア on the left edge; headline bottom-left; vermilion CTA bottom-right; "cambiar diseño" shuffle under the shirt.
FORM: manga chapter scroll, 1st on list. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction
Scroll scrubs the camera around the shirt across four panels (prenda, diseño, tamaño real con reglas en cm, impresión DTF); the shuffle swaps the print with a screentone wipe.
